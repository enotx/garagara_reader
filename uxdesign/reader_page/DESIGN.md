## Brand & Style

The design system is grounded in the tangible, tactile intimacy of physical editorial print: **"Paper and ink, with a highlighter"**. Designed specifically for foreign-language immersion reading, it strips away aggressive gamification, hyperactive notification badges, and fluorescent feedback loops in favor of quiet, literary restraint. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of classical bookbinding and purposeful utilitarian minimalism. The interface acts not as an attention merchant, but as an invisible substrate that supports deep immersion. Reading content is sacred and immutable; tools, glosses, and lexicographical insights float gently over the text without jostling or reflowing the reading canvas. 

### Visual Tenets
- **Editorial Subservience**: UI chrome remains invisible until summoned. Controls, chapter headers, and pagination cues sit low on visual hierarchy.
- **Semantic Tangibility**: Color is reserved solely for cognitive reading affordances: **Ink Blue** for deliberate interactive touches, **Highlighter Ochre** for focused linguistic comprehension, and **Cinnabar Stamp** for collecting words into long-term memory.
- **Atmospheric Warmth**: Even in digital space, paper has fiber and warmth. High-contrast harsh whites are replaced by aged rag paper and warm bone tones.

## Colors

The palette establishes four perceptual modes:
1. **Light / Paper (Default)**: Warm rag paper (`#FAF8F3`) paired with dense charcoal-ink typography (`#1F1D1A`).
2. **Eye-Care / Warm Paper**: Amber-tinted background (`#F1E7D0`) designed to minimize eye fatigue during extended twilight reading sessions.
3. **Dark / Night**: Deep, unlit soot (`#141312`) with softened alabaster glyphs (`#E6E1D8`) that preserve dark adaptation without glare.
4. **E-Ink Native Mode**: Absolute 1-bit high-contrast black (`#000000`) and pure white (`#FFFFFF`), replacing tints with structural outlines and inverted fills.

### Semantic Color Rules
- **Primary Ink Blue (`#2E5A87`)**: Anchors active interaction, audio pronunciation triggers, audio wave indicators, and primary callouts.
- **Highlighter Ochre (`#EFE3C2`)**: The visual equivalent of a felt-tip highlighter laid across printed paper. Used exclusively to bind attention to current sentence chunks, clauses, and Focus reading segments.
- **Word Selection Blue (`#DCE4EC`)**: Soft tint for active lexical selection and multi-word idioms.
- **Cinnabar Stamp (`#B5452E`)**: Reserved strictly for the act of collection and marking—mimicking a traditional seal stamped into book margins for saved items and flashcard additions.
- **Dimmed Text (`#8C867C`)**: Applied during Focus Mode to de-emphasize peripheral sentences, maintaining a readable yet subdued 3.4:1 contrast ratio.

## Typography

The typographical architecture strictly distinguishes between **L2 Source Text (The Material)** and **L1 Explanatory Apparatus (The Commentary)**:

- **Source Literature**: Rendered in `Literata` (with `Source Serif 4` as approved secondary fallback). The reading body is calibrated to 18px with an expansive line height of 1.7 (`30.6px`) to invite sustained cognitive reading and clear eye return.
- **Apparatus & Dictionary Layers**: Rendered in crisp, neutral sans-serif (`Inter` fallbacking to platform system stacks `PingFang SC` / `-apple-system`). Translations, part-of-speech tags, phonetic guides, and controls reside strictly in this modern register, creating an unmistakable boundary between the original literature and analytical help.
- **Numbers & Metadata**: All counters, page positions, audio timestamps, and percentages use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal layout jank during active pagination or audio scrubbing.

## Layout & Spacing

Reading space demands mathematical stability. Content dimensions must never dance or jitter when bottom sheets or toolbars manifest.

### Layout Principles
- **Base Rhythm**: Built on a strict 4px / 8px incremental scale.
- **Touch Target Integrity**: Interactive targets observe a non-negotiable minimum area of 44 × 44px regardless of visual glyph size.
- **The Reading Sanctuary**:
  - **Phone (393 × 852 baseline)**: Lateral reading margin set to `1.5rem` (24px).
  - **Tablet Portrait**: Reading measure capped at 60–75 characters per line (approx. 560–640px column width) with widened automatic side gutters.
  - **Tablet Landscape & Desktop**: Multi-column book spread or anchored master-detail canvas, where definitions appear in a stationary side rail rather than an overlapping sheet.
- **Overlay Decoupling**: All floating lexicographical sheets and translation badges layer strictly over the content canvas via fixed positioning. The underlying paginated layout is never compressed or reflowed upon user interaction.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and minimal, drawing from card stock resting upon heavy book paper rather than glowing digital surfaces.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Canvas (`paper-bg`)**: Base ground. Zero shadow, matte, non-reflective.
2. **Floating Sheets & Popovers (`surface`)**: 
   - Border: 1px continuous hairline in `#E6E1D6`.
   - Shadow: Extremely diffuse, low-contrast ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 29, 26, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 29, 26, 0.04)`.
3. **Dark Mode Elevation**: Drop shadows are eliminated entirely to avoid artificial luminescence. Depth is communicated strictly via tonal stepped contrast: `#1E1C1A` surface resting on `#141312` background, framed by a `1px solid #2C2926` divider line.
4. **E-Ink Mode Elevation**: 0px blur, 0px shadow. Floating surfaces are delineated entirely with a `1.5px` crisp solid `#000000` outline.

## Shapes

The shape system balances soft, approachable floating sheets with disciplined reading controls.

- **Floating Panels & Bottom Sheets**: Rendered with generous 16px corner radii (`1rem`), mimicking thick, rounded recipe cards and bookplates.
- **Buttons, Chips & Inputs**: Standardized at 10px (`0.625rem`) corner radii, preserving finger-friendly softness without falling into playful novelty.
- **E-Ink Exception**: For E-Ink modes, all corner radii flatten globally to a crisp 4px (`0.25rem`) to eliminate jagged anti-aliasing artifacts common to electrophoretic displays.

## Components

### 1. Word Lookup & Definition Cards
- **Base**: Grounded in `surface` with 16px border-radius, bounded by 1px `#E6E1D6`. Default height spans maximum 40% of viewport, expandable to full screen via vertical drag.
- **Lemma Header**: Lemma rendered in `definition-title` (Literata 20px Bold), paired adjacent to phonetic text in `meta-numeric` (12px neutral text-secondary) and an audio speaker button (1.5px Lucide stroke, 44x44px target).
- **In-Context Translation Badge**: Labeled with a flat, muted chip `"本句含义"` (Sans-Serif 11px, `#FAF8F3` fill, 1px `#E6E1D6` border). Free of superfluous AI sparkle decorations.
- **Actions**: Bookmark toggle button with `#B5452E` Cinnabar star icon (`☆` hollow default, `★` filled when collected).

### 2. Interactive Buttons
- **Primary / Action**: Solid `#2E5A87` fill with white text. 10px corner radius, height 44px, horizontal padding `space-lg`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px `#E6E1D6` border, `#1F1D1A` text. Hover/pressed state shifts background to `#FAF8F3`.
- **Icon Controls (Header/Footer)**: Transparent 44 × 44px boundary, rendering 1.5px linear vector glyphs in `#6B665E`. Active state shifts icon fill to `#2E5A87`.

### 3. Reading Highlighter & Selection Underlays
- **Word Selection**: Words tapped for lookup receive a rounded rectangular underlay fill of `#DCE4EC` with 4px border radius.
- **Focus Segment**: Continuous sentence focus underlaid with Highlighter Ochre `#EFE3C2` (Eye-Care: `#E6D3A8`; Dark: `#3A3324`). In E-Ink mode, this falls back to a 1.5px solid border outline or a 2px underline.
- **De-emphasized Text**: Sentences outside the immediate focus segment transition their color to `#8C867C` at 150ms ease-out.

### 4. Floating Translation Subtitle Strip
- **Container**: Compact pill-card floating at the bottom third of the reading pane. Max-width 90% of reading column, centered.
- **Typography**: Dual-line presentation—primary source segment in 14px Sans-Serif regular, translated meaning in 13px `#6B665E`.
- **Dismiss & Controls**: Audio replay toggle and translation toggle (`译`) housed within 36px circular micro-buttons.

### 5. Chips & Morphological Tags
- **Appearance**: Flat tags indicating verb forms (e.g., `took → take [v.]`), rendered with 4px radius, 4px vertical / 8px horizontal padding, `#FAF8F3` background, and 1px `#E6E1D6` border. Text in `label-sm` (`#6B665E`).
